## 1. Central Scientific Hypothesis

> **Hypothesis**: Training a deep facial de-occlusion network on photo-realistic synthetic occluded faces generated by latent diffusion inpainting—providing mathematically aligned clean-occluded-mask triplets $(I_{\text{clean}}, I_{\text{occ}}, M_{\text{occ}})$ with physically accurate contact shadows and 3D facial conformity—significantly improves reconstruction fidelity, identity preservation, and perceptual realism ($\ge +2.5\text{ dB}$ PSNR, $\ge +0.06$ SSIM, $\ge -0.05$ LPIPS, $\ge +12\%$ ArcFace ID similarity) on real-world occluded portraits compared to models trained on traditional 2D Cut-and-Paste synthetic data.

## 3. Unseen Test Benchmark Isolation

To guarantee scientific integrity:
1. **Zero Data Contamination**: No images from the evaluation benchmarks (AR Face Dataset paired split / RealOcc test split) are ever exposed to diffusion prompt conditioning, attention extraction, or training pipelines.
2. **Gold Standard Test Split**: Exactly 1,000 annotated paired holdout portraits and real-world test images.
3. **Fixed Random Seed**: Global seed `seed = 42` locked across data loaders and model weight initialization.

---

## 4. Ablation Studies

To isolate the contributions of individual system components, three ablation series are scheduled:

### 4.1 Ablation A: Contribution of Loss Components
- Condition A1: $L_1$ reconstruction loss alone ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{hole}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{valid}}$).
- Condition A2: $L_1$ + Perceptual Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{perc}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{style}}$).
- Condition A3 (Full): $L_1$ + Perceptual + ArcFace Identity Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{id}}$).
- *Metric*: Measure PSNR, LPIPS, and ArcFace Identity Similarity across conditions.

### 4.2 Ablation B: Dataset Volume Scaling
- Train restoration network on synthetic pools of size $N \in \{1,000, 2,500, 5,000, 8,000, 12,000\}$.
- Plot the scaling curve of PSNR and SSIM vs. synthetic sample count.

### 4.3 Ablation C: Triplet Generation Fidelity (Diffusion vs Cut-and-Paste)
- Compare downstream de-occlusion performance on real-world occluded faces when trained on Diffusion triplets vs 2D Cut-and-Paste triplets.

## 5. Statistical Significance & Reporting Standards

1. **Multiple Runs**: Every primary experiment (EXP-0 through EXP-3) must be executed over 3 distinct random seeds ($42, 1337, 2024$).
2. **Reporting Format**:
   - Values presented as $\text{Mean} \pm \text{Std Dev}$.
   - Class-wise breakdown table for all 5 foreground occlusion classes.
   - Downstream biometric face verification accuracy curves (ROC / AUC).
3. **Artifact Logging**:
   - Metrics logged in standard JSON format: `experiments/{exp_id}/metrics.json`.
   - Qualitative visual galleries (`[Occluded | Mask | Restored | Clean GT | Error Map]`) saved as `.png` visual artifacts under `experiments/predictions/`.
